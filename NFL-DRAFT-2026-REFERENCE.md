## QB Rankings

### QB1: Fernando Mendoza — Indiana (formerly Cal)
- **Consensus #1 overall pick to the Raiders. LOCKED.**
- Heisman Trophy winner
- Led Indiana to their FIRST national championship
- Stats: 3,535 passing yards, 41 TDs, 6 INTs
- Iconic moments: game-winning drive at Oregon, 80-yard game-winning drive at Penn State, overcame getting knocked out vs Ohio State in Big Ten championship, game-winning TD run in national title game vs Miami
- Known for: Toughness, clutch performance, pocket poise
- Weakness: Struggles when forced off his launch point (53.2% completion when moving)
- Kirk Cousins signed as bridge QB — $20M guaranteed for 2026, Falcons paying most of it. Cousins worked with Raiders HC Klint Kubiak in Minnesota.

### QB2: Ty Simpson — Alabama
- First year as full-time starter, only 15 career college starts
- Tale of two halves: Weeks 1-8 = 18 TDs, 1 INT, 276 yards/game. Final 8 games = 10 TDs, 11 turnover-worthy plays, 204 yards/game
- Expected to go late 1st round — only other QB projected for round 1
- Real question: which team takes the swing on him and where?

### QB3-4: Day 2 Tier
- **Garrett Nussmeier (LSU)** — Regressed badly. 1,927 yards, 12 TDs, 5 INTs in 9 games in 2025.
- **Carson Beck (Georgia)** — In the same Day 2 tier with starter upside.

## Top Non-QB Prospects

### Jeremiyah Love — RB, Notre Dame
- CLEAR #1 RB in the class, not close
- Doak Walker Award winner (best college RB)
- 35 rushing TDs, 40 total TDs over 2024-2025 seasons
- 4.36 speed, excellent vision, run-you-over power, soft hands as a receiver
- 6th player in FBS history with multiple seasons of 6.5+ YPC and 15+ rushing TDs
- Multiple mocks have him top 5 (Kiper has him to Titans at #4)
- Only debate: Is a top-5 RB pick worth it in today's NFL?

### Arvell Reese — LB/Edge, Ohio State
- Draft stock SKYROCKETED during 2025 season
- Some scouts call him the best overall player in the class
- Uber-athletic, 86.5 PFF run-defense grade
- Was relatively unknown a year ago — now a potential top-3 pick

## Kirk Cousins to Raiders — Key Details
- 5-year, $172M contract — looks big but heavily structured
- Only $20M fully guaranteed for 2026 season
- Raiders only pay $1.3M — Falcons paying the rest (dead cap from ATL release)
- Another $10M guaranteed due March 2027
- Cousins and HC Klint Kubiak worked together in Minnesota (Kubiak was his QB coach → OC)
- This is explicitly a bridge/mentor role for Mendoza
- Smart fantasy angle: Cousins is a competent passer → Brock Bowers target upside stays elite
