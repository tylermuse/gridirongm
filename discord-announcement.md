hey everyone — big update just went live. this one's all about making the game feel deeper without adding bloat. here's what's new:

**Your job is on the line now**
There's an owner watching you. You'll see two new gauges on your dashboard — Fan Pulse and Owner Confidence. Win games, they go up. Lose ugly, they drop. The owner also sets 2-3 objectives at the start of each season (things like "win 9 games" or "make the playoffs" or "develop your young QB"). Hit them and you're golden. Miss them and you'll get a warning. Miss them again and you're fired — for real. You can take a job with another team in the same league or walk away. Makes every decision feel like it actually matters now.

**Scouting got way more interesting**
The draft isn't just "sort by projected OVR and pick the best guy" anymore. You now have scout points to spend on different intel — send a scout trip to narrow down a prospect's rating and get a strength/weakness report, sit down for an interview to read their personality and maybe sniff out a bust, or attend a pro day to see one of their hidden ratings in action. You get 10-20 points per draft depending on your scouting level, so you have to be strategic about who you invest in. Makes the late rounds way more fun.

**Coaches have a pulse**
Coaches already had schemes and traits, but now they actually evolve. Good coaches get better over time, bad ones decline. They retire when they get old. And the big one — AI teams will actually fire their head coach after bad seasons. You'll see coaching carousel news during the offseason now. 75% chance a coach gets axed after a 4-win season, and there's a 40% chance the coordinators go with them. The league feels more alive season to season.

**Rivalry games hit different**
Rivalry intensity now actually does something on the field. Higher intensity means more fumbles, more chaos. And AI GMs aren't stupid about it anymore — they won't trade their stars to a rival. That alone makes divisional races feel more real.

**Live games got a glow-up**
There's a momentum system running under the hood now. Touchdowns, turnovers, sacks, big plays — they all shift momentum between -100 and +100, and it actually affects how the next few plays go. The play-by-play text is a lot more varied too. More templates, more situational awareness. Less repetitive, more fun to watch.

that's the update. lmk what you think — feedback always welcome. more stuff coming soon.
