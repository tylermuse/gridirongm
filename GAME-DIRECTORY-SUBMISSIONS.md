## Short Description (1-2 sentences)
BS Football is a free browser-based NFL GM simulator where you manage any franchise's roster, salary cap, draft, and trades to build a dynasty. No downloads required — jump in and start your first season in 30 seconds.

## Medium Description (~100 words)
BS Football is a deep NFL front office simulator that puts you in the GM chair of any franchise. Manage the salary cap, scout and draft rookies, sign free agents, make blockbuster trades, and navigate the full NFL season — all from your browser with zero downloads. Every decision matters: overpay a star and you'll be stuck in cap hell, nail a draft and you'll build a dynasty. The game features realistic roster management, a full salary cap system, and season-long simulation. Free to play, with an optional Pro tier for power users who want extra save slots and features.

## Long Description (~250 words)
BS Football puts you in the general manager's seat of any NFL franchise. This isn't a casual football game — it's a full front office simulation where every roster decision, cap move, and draft pick shapes your team's future.

**Core Gameplay:**
- Take over any of 32 NFL franchises with realistic rosters
- Manage the salary cap — overspend and face the consequences
- Scout and draft rookies each offseason
- Sign free agents and negotiate contracts
- Execute trades with AI-controlled teams
- Simulate full NFL seasons and chase the championship

**What Makes It Different:**
Unlike mobile NFL games loaded with microtransactions, BS Football is a pure strategy experience that runs entirely in your browser. No app downloads, no account required to start playing. The game saves automatically to your browser, so you can pick up your franchise anytime.

The depth is real: you'll deal with aging veterans, rookie contracts, cap casualties, and the temptation to mortgage your future for a win-now move. Every decision has tradeoffs, just like running a real NFL team.

**Perfect For:**
- Fantasy football players who want more control
- NFL fans who think they could run a team better than their GM
- Fans of management sims like Football Manager or Out of the Park Baseball
- Anyone who wants a deep strategy game they can play in a browser tab at work

Free to play. No downloads. Start your dynasty at bs-football.com.

## 1. CrazyGames (developer.crazygames.com)

**Steps:**
1. Go to https://developer.crazygames.com
2. Create developer account
3. Submit game via their developer portal
4. They'll review and potentially feature it

**Notes:**
- CrazyGames takes a revenue share but drives significant traffic
- They prefer games with their SDK integrated (optional but helps ranking)
- Upload format: They accept URL-based games (just point to bs-football.com) or upload a ZIP
- Review typically takes 1-2 weeks
- Use the "Medium Description" above for their submission form

## 2. itch.io

**Steps:**
1. Go to https://itch.io/register
2. Create account
3. Go to https://itch.io/game/new
4. Set "Kind of project" → HTML / Web
5. For "Upload" → you can either embed via iframe or upload a build
6. Set pricing to "Free" or "$0 or donate"

**Recommended Settings:**
- Classification: Game
- Kind of project: HTML
- Embed options: Click to launch in a new window → link to bs-football.com
- Release status: Released
- Pricing: $0 or donate
- Community: Enable comments
- Tags: nfl, football, gm-simulator, management, strategy, sports, simulation, browser
- Genre: Simulation, Strategy, Sports

**Notes:**
- itch.io has a strong indie community
- Games with good thumbnails/screenshots get more clicks
- Consider creating a devlog post about the game
- Use the "Long Description" above formatted in markdown
